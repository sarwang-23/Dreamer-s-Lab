BECOME A MENTOR

Your experience could change someone’s trajectory.

You don’t need to commit to running an entire program to make a difference. You might lead a workshop, offer a focused mentoring session, review a pitch, share an industry perspective or help a founder think through a specific challenge. As a Building Dreams mentor, you may contribute through:

Guest sessions & masterclasses
Founder office hours
Product & business reviews
Pitch feedback & refinement
Industry introductions
Student project reviews
Demo days & cohort showcase events

We value mentors who are practical, constructive, respectful and willing to share lessons from both successes and setbacks.

COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Building Dreams is a place to build together.

Building Dreams is intended to be a constructive environment where people can ask questions, exchange ideas, share feedback and learn from one another. We expect community members to:

Treat others with respect.
Give honest, constructive feedback.
Respect confidential business information.
Credit original work and ideas.
Avoid misleading claims & unsolicited promotions.
Honour agreed commitments.
Respect student privacy and safeguarding requirements.
