The mentor network

Experience that makes a difference.

Entrepreneurship often requires knowledge that goes beyond a textbook. A mentor can help a founder understand a market, question a business model, improve a product or avoid an unnecessary detour. We aim to bring together mentors across:

  • Startup founding and leadership.
  • Product management and technology.
  • Marketing, branding and customer acquisition.
  • Sales, partnerships and distribution.
  • Finance, accounting and business operations.
  • Legal and compliance fundamentals.
  • Fundraising and investor readiness.
  • Industry-specific knowledge.
  • Career and entrepreneurial development.

Mentors are matched to the needs and stage of a program wherever possible.

Become a mentor

Your experience could change someone’s trajectory.

You don’t need to commit to running an entire program to make a difference. You might lead a workshop, offer a focused mentoring session, review a pitch, share an industry perspective or help a founder think through a specific challenge. As a Building Dreams mentor, you may contribute through:

  • Guest sessions and masterclasses.
  • Founder office hours.
  • Product and business reviews.
  • Pitch feedback.
  • Industry introductions where appropriate.
  • Student project reviews.
  • Demo days and showcase events.

We value mentors who are practical, constructive, respectful and willing to share lessons from both successes and setbacks.

Community guidelines

Building Dreams is intended to be a constructive environment…

…where people can ask questions, exchange ideas, share feedback and learn from one another. We expect community members to:

  • Treat others with respect.
  • Give honest, constructive feedback.
  • Respect confidential business information.
  • Credit original work and ideas.
  • Avoid misleading claims and unsolicited promotions.
  • Honour agreed commitments.
  • Respect student privacy and safeguarding requirements.
