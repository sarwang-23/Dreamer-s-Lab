Our Venture-Building Pathway

From an idea to your first real customers.

  • 01

    Discover

    Understand your interests, identify problems and select a promising opportunity.

  • 02

    Validate

    Talk to prospective customers, examine alternatives and test whether the problem matters.

  • 03

    Design

    Define the solution, user experience, business model and minimum set of features.

  • 04

    Build

    Develop a prototype or MVP that allows you to test your key assumptions.

  • 05

    Launch

    Find your first users through outreach, partnerships, communities and targeted experiments.

  • 06

    Grow

    Use feedback and early evidence to improve the product, sales process and business model.

  • 07

    Prepare for the next stage

    Decide whether to continue experimenting, pursue revenue, join an incubator or explore fundraising.

HOW YOU WILL WORK

Build in focused sprints.

Rather than spending months discussing an idea without testing it, participants work through structured milestones. Each sprint has:

    01

    A clear objective.

  1. 02

    A practical task or experiment.

  2. 03

    A measurable output.

  3. 04

    Mentor or peer feedback.

  4. 05

    A decision about what to do next.

SPRINT EXAMPLE

Example: a customer-discovery sprint

A customer-discovery sprint may focus on interviewing ten relevant potential users, identifying recurring problems and deciding whether to continue, refine or change the idea.

The numbers and milestones will be tailored to each venture rather than treated as universal success criteria.
