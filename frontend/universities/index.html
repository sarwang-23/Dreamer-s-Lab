Awareness

Inspire students to explore entrepreneurship across all campus departments.

Discovery

Identify problems, personal curiosities, and high-potential venture ideas.

Validate problems with customers and build working early prototypes.

Incubation

Support promising ventures as they refine products and business models.

Help eligible startups strengthen traction, pilots, and prepare for growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Working with institutions.

Common questions from university leadership, deans, and incubator directors.

Does Building Dreams replace our existing incubation centre?

Not necessarily. We can complement existing facilities by supporting early-stage founder discovery, pre-incubation, structured cohorts or specialist delivery.

Can the program be customised?

Yes. Programs can be tailored by academic discipline, student level, sector, duration and institutional objectives.

Can faculty members participate?

Yes. Faculty can be involved in mentoring, evaluation, workshops and relevant subject-matter guidance.

Can you support a university with no existing startup ecosystem?

We can help design an initial entrepreneurship pathway and progressively develop the required programs and networks.

Do you guarantee startup creation or investment?

No. We focus on the quality of the process, founder progress and measurable program outcomes. Startup creation and investment depend on many factors beyond the program.
