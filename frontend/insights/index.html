Founder stories

Real journeys. Honest lessons.

As Building Dreams programs develop, publish stories about participants who have:

  • Discovered a problem worth solving.
  • Changed their original idea after customer research.
  • Built their first prototype.
  • Run their first pilot.
  • Acquired their first customer.
  • Learned from a failed experiment.
  • Progressed into an incubator or accelerator.

Prioritise honest accounts of progress and learning over exaggerated success stories.
