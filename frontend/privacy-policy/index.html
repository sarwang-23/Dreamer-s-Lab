Student Safeguarding & Regulatory Alignment

Because Dreamers Lab serves school-age students (Grades 9–12), Building Dreams implements dedicated child privacy and parental consent processes before collecting student information. This policy is aligned with Indian data-protection requirements (Digital Personal Data Protection Act) and global best practices.

CLAUSE 01

What personal information the website collects

When you interact with Building Dreams through our website, application forms, or enquiry channels, we may collect personal details you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Contact & Identity Data: Full name, email address, phone number, and location.
  • Student & Parent Details: Student name, school, current grade, and parent/guardian contact credentials.
  • Founder & Venture Profiles: University affiliation, course of study, startup idea name, pitch deck links, and problem statements.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, device type, browser specifications, and anonymous page interaction telemetry used exclusively to maintain site performance and security.
CLAUSE 02

Why contact and application information is collected

We process personal and venture data solely for legitimate educational, operational, and cohort management objectives:

  • To evaluate program applications and match applicants to suitable cohort tracks.
  • To facilitate communication, onboarding interviews, and cohort orientation.
  • To answer questions from students, parents, universities, and prospective mentors.
  • To deliver mentorship sessions, venture feedback, and institutional progress reporting.

We only collect data that is strictly relevant to these purposes. We never repurpose applicant information for unrelated commercial advertising.

CLAUSE 03

How applications and enquiries are stored and used

All submitted applications and contact requests are stored in encrypted, access-controlled databases and enterprise submission environments.

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards against loss, unauthorised alteration, disclosure, or breach. Information is retained only as long as necessary to administer active cohort cycles and provide ongoing alumni or partnership support, after which records are securely purged or anonymised.

CLAUSE 04

Who can access that information

Access is restricted strictly on a need-to-know basis:

  • Authorised members of the Building Dreams admissions and venture team.
  • Assigned mentors and evaluators who review specific application files under non-disclosure obligations.

Zero Commercial Sale: Building Dreams will never sell, rent, monetize, or trade applicant personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies.

CLAUSE 05

Third-party analytics, form providers, and CRM systems

To operate our digital ecosystem reliably, Building Dreams may employ trusted third-party cloud infrastructure, such as secure form management endpoints, encrypted CRM pipelines, and privacy-first analytics tools.

All third-party service providers undergo vetting to ensure compliance with modern data encryption standards (TLS 1.3, AES-256) and contractual data processing agreements that prohibit secondary usage.

CLAUSE 06

How users can request access, correction, or deletion

In accordance with data protection legislation, you retain full ownership of your personal data:

  • Access & Portability: You may request a summary of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can update or correct outdated contact or project credentials at any time.
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request that we delete your application records or contact details when you no longer participate in our programs.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@buildingdreams.in.

CLAUSE 07

How minors’ information and parent/guardian consent are handled

✦ Parental Consent Requirement for Dreamers Lab

For any applicant under the age of 18, explicit parent or guardian verification is mandatory. Application forms collect parent/guardian email and telephone details so our team can directly confirm consent before cohort enrolment.

We do not collect unnecessary personal details from school students. Parents or legal guardians may review, update, or revoke consent for their child’s participation and request immediate data deletion at any stage.
