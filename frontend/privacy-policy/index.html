Privacy Policy.

This policy explains what personal information the Building Dreams website collects, why it is collected and how it is handled.

Last updated: [date to be confirmed]

This policy is a working draft. Because Dreamers Lab serves school-age students, implement appropriate privacy and safeguarding processes before collecting student information, and have the final policy reviewed for applicable Indian data-protection requirements.

Information we collect

When you interact with the Building Dreams website, we may collect personal information that you choose to provide. This can include your name, email address, phone number and other contact details, as well as details you share when you submit an enquiry, register an interest or complete an application for our programs.

We may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type and pages visited, to help us understand how the website is used and to keep it working reliably.

Why we collect it

We collect contact and application information so that we can respond to your enquiries, share relevant information about our programs and process applications or registrations. It also helps us understand who is interested in our work so we can improve what we offer.

We only collect information that is relevant to these purposes and we do not use your details for unrelated reasons without your consent.

How it is stored and used

Applications and enquiries are stored in controlled systems and used only for the purposes outlined in this policy. We take reasonable steps to protect this information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and we retain it only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected.

When information is no longer required, we take steps to delete or anonymise it in line with our internal retention practices.

Who can access it

Access to personal information is limited to members of the Building Dreams team and authorised partners who need it to respond to your enquiry or manage a program. These individuals are expected to handle information responsibly and confidentially.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-party services

We may use third-party services to support the website and our operations, such as analytics tools, form providers or customer relationship (CRM) systems. Where these services process personal information on our behalf, we aim to use providers that maintain appropriate privacy and security standards.

The use of any such service is limited to helping us operate the website, communicate with you and manage enquiries and applications.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct information that is inaccurate, or request that we delete information where there is no ongoing reason for us to keep it.

To make a request, please contact us using the details in the section below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and may ask you to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Minors and consent

Some of our programs and the Dreamers Lab serve school-age students. Where we collect information about a minor, we expect it to be provided or approved by a parent or legal guardian, and we handle such information with additional care.

We encourage parents and guardians to be involved in any decision to share a minor’s information with us, and to contact us if they have questions or wish to withdraw consent.

Contact us

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to privacy, please contact Building Dreams by email or through our contact page. We will do our best to address your query promptly.
