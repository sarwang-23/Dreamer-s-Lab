01 / Explore Grades 10–12

Dreamers Lab

“Turn curiosity into your first venture.”

₹ 3,499 / 2 months

Complete program fee • No recurring charges

Duration: 2 months
Format: Live online classes every Sunday at 11:30 AM
Cohort Size: Small cohort, max 20 students

An engaging, practical introduction to entrepreneurship. Students learn to think like founders, explore problems worth solving and work in a guided environment to develop and present an initial business idea.

What’s Included
  • ✓ Two-month live online learning experience
  • ✓ Guided activities and practical assignments
  • ✓ Peer learning in a small cohort (maximum 20 students)
  • ✓ Final venture presentation or Demo Day
Who It Is For

Students in Grades 10–12 who are curious about entrepreneurship, want to solve real-world problems, or would like to explore building a business while still at school.

What Students Will Explore
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, creativity and opportunity spotting.
  • Identifying real problems and understanding the people who experience them.
  • Idea development, simple customer research and basic validation.
  • Business models, value propositions and how a business can earn revenue.
  • Creating a simple prototype or MVP and collecting feedback.
  • Storytelling, presentation skills and pitching a business idea.
Expected Takeaways
  • A developed business idea with initial validation.
  • A basic prototype or MVP concept.
  • A simple business model and founder pitch presentation.
  • Greater confidence in creative problem-solving and presenting ideas.
Program Outcome

Students finish with a practical first step into entrepreneurship—not just theoretical knowledge. Any certificate will be issued according to the program’s completion criteria.

02 / Build College & Early Founders

Dreamers Cohort

“From business idea to a clear startup action plan.”

₹ 3,999 / 2 months

Complete group cohort fee • High-interaction model

Duration: 2 months
Format: Live online classes every Sunday at 11:30 AM
Cohort Size: Max 20 participants for feedback

A two-month, live online group program that takes participants through the core stages of an early venture—from identifying a meaningful problem to validating an idea, planning an MVP and understanding how to reach early customers.

What’s Included
  • ✓ Two months of live online group sessions on Sundays at 11:30 AM
  • ✓ Maximum of 20 participants to support interaction and feedback
  • ✓ Practical assignments and milestone-based progress
  • ✓ Peer learning, founder discussions and showcase presentations
Who It Is For

College students and aspiring entrepreneurs who want a structured introduction to building a startup, learn alongside peers and make progress on a business idea.

Core Learning Journey
  • Idea discovery: identify a customer problem and define the target audience.
  • Customer discovery & validation: conduct conversations, test assumptions and assess demand.
  • Business model: define the value proposition, revenue model and basic economics.
  • MVP planning: decide what to build first and how to test it without overbuilding.
  • Go-to-market: understand positioning, early customer acquisition and launch planning.
  • Pitching & investor readiness: communicate problem, solution, market and business model; understand startup funding basics.
Expected Takeaways
  • A clearer, evidence-informed business idea.
  • A basic business model and validation learnings.
  • An MVP scope and initial go-to-market plan.
  • A founder pitch outline and next-step roadmap.
Program Outcome

Participants leave with a clearer understanding of their startup opportunity and a practical roadmap for the next stage. Transparent policy: Investor introductions, funding and commercial outcomes are not guaranteed.

Dreamers 1:1 Mentorship

Duration: 2 months
Format: 8 private sessions × 60 minutes
Advisory: 1-on-1 tailored founder attention

Offers a personalised two-month journey. The sessions are tailored to the founder’s stage, challenges and goals while covering the core entrepreneurial journey—from idea validation and MVP planning to go-to-market strategy and investor readiness.

What’s Included
  • ✓ Eight one-on-one mentoring sessions of 60 minutes each over two months
  • ✓ Guidance strictly tailored to the founder’s idea and venture stage
  • ✓ Action points and milestone reviews to support relentless execution
  • ✓ Fundraising strategy guidance and relevant investor connections where appropriate
Who It Is For

Aspiring entrepreneurs and early-stage founders who want individual attention, tailored feedback and accountability as they develop and test a business idea.

The Eight-Session Journey
  • Session 1: Founder goals, idea review and opportunity framing.
  • Session 2: Customer problem, target audience and discovery plan.
  • Session 3: Idea validation, customer feedback and key assumptions.
  • Session 4: Business model, value proposition and monetisation.
  • Session 5: MVP scope, priorities and development roadmap.
  • Session 6: Go-to-market strategy and early customer acquisition.
  • Session 7: Pitch narrative, business story and investor readiness.
  • Session 8: Progress review, priorities and next-stage action plan.
Expected Takeaways
  • A prioritised plan for validating the opportunity.
  • A business model and MVP roadmap.
  • A practical go-to-market plan.
  • A pitch narrative and investor-readiness checklist.
  • Clear next steps for continued execution.
Program Outcome

Mentoring, guidance and introductions where appropriate—not a guarantee of funding or commercial success. Scheduling and between-session support are agreed upfront.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about enrolment, payments, sessions, and outcomes.

Which program is right for me? +

Dreamers Lab (₹3,499) is designed for high school students (Grades 10–12) wanting an engaging, guided introduction to founder thinking.

Dreamers Cohort (₹3,999) is tailored for college students and aspiring entrepreneurs ready to validate business models, plan MVPs, and prepare startup pitches.

Dreamers 1:1 Mentorship (₹8,999) provides 8 private, highly tailored 60-minute sessions for founders who want focused advisory, accountability, and customized roadmaps.

Do I need an existing business idea to join? +

No prior business knowledge or finished idea is required. In Dreamers Lab and Dreamers Cohort, the first modules focus specifically on opportunity spotting, problem discovery, and uncovering ideas worth pursuing.

How are live online Sunday sessions delivered? +

Live classes take place every Sunday at 11:30 AM via interactive video meeting. Because cohorts are capped at 20 participants, sessions are highly conversational with breakout exercises, live feedback, and real founder discussions.

What happens if I miss a live class? +

All group sessions are recorded and made accessible in the participant portal alongside session presentation slides and activity templates. For 1:1 Mentorship, private sessions can be rescheduled directly with your assigned mentor.

Does Building Dreams invest in participants or guarantee funding? +

Building Dreams operates as an education and venture-building platform. While we provide fundraising strategy guidance and relevant investor connections where appropriate, funding and investor outcomes depend on merit, market demand, and founder effort, and are not guaranteed.

Can parents or schools sponsor student enrolments? +

Yes. Parents frequently enrol high school students in Dreamers Lab. Schools and colleges also partner with us to sponsor dedicated cohorts for their student entrepreneurship cells. Reach out through our contact page for institutional arrangements.
