✦ Application Roadmap

What happens next?

Here is how our cohort review and onboarding process works from application to launch.

STEP 01

Application Review

Our mentorship team reviews your background, curiosity, and starting point against the upcoming cohort milestones.

STEP 02

Exploratory Conversation

A brief, informal 15-minute conversation with a mentor to align on goals, schedule, and team fit.

STEP 03

Cohort Invitation

Accepted founders receive their cohort calendar, sprint access, and orientation toolkit to start building.
