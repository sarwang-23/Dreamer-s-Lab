THE 12-WEEK DREAMERS LAB JOURNEY
05 PHASES · 12 WEEKS

One cohort. Twelve weeks. A journey from curiosity to creation.

From discovering meaningful problems to building and presenting ideas, students learn through a structured journey of exploration, experimentation, and feedback.

PHASE 01 OF 05
01
Weeks 1–2 · Foundation
Milestone: Hypotheses

Phase 1 — Discover

“Understand yourself. Explore the world.”

Students identify their interests, strengths and curiosities, explore entrepreneurial thinking and learn to notice problems worth solving in their schools and communities.

Phase Deliverables
✓Personal interest map ✓Observation exercise ✓Initial problem ideas
→
02
Weeks 3–4 · Exploration
Milestone: User Profile

Phase 2 — Ideate

“Find a problem worth solving.”

Students explore different problems, select a target audience, brainstorm solutions and narrow their ideas using simple evaluation frameworks and decision matrices.

Phase Deliverables
✓Problem statement ✓Target-user profile ✓Initial solution concept
→
03
Weeks 5–6 · Testing
Milestone: Validation

Phase 3 — Validate

“Talk to people. Test assumptions.”

Students learn to ask better questions, conduct simple customer interviews, understand existing market alternatives and gather real feedback before writing code or building hardware.

Phase Deliverables
✓Interview insights ✓Problem validation summary ✓Refined idea blueprint
→
04
Weeks 7–9 · Studio Build
Milestone: V1.0 Prototype

Phase 4 — Build

“Bring the idea to life.”

Students create a low-cost prototype, physical mockup, service demonstration or simple digital product. They test with users, receive direct mentor critique, and iterate their solution.

Phase Deliverables
✓Working prototype ✓Solution demonstration ✓User feedback log
→
05
Weeks 10–12 · Demo Day
Grand Finale: Demo Day

Phase 5 — Pitch & Present

“Share what you built. Show what you learned.”

Students refine their business model, estimate basic operational costs, prepare a high-impact pitch deck, and present their venture live to mentors, school leaders, peers, and invited guests.

Phase Deliverables
✓Final pitch deck ✓Live demonstration ✓Next-steps roadmap
